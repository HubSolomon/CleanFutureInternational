What CFI will not do
A model is defined as much by its refusals as by its ambitions. Each of these describes a decision that is easy to make under pressure — a deadline, a funding window, a photograph.
Project red lines
×
CFI will not build first and solve maintenance later.
×
CFI will not assume donor funding will permanently cover operating costs.
×
CFI will not assume user fees automatically make infrastructure profitable.
×
CFI will not treat construction completion as proof of impact.
×
CFI will not describe a project as sustainable without evidence.
Funding red lines
×
CFI will not accept restricted funding that leaves core costs unfunded.
×
CFI will not build an asset without a funded Layer 2 plan.
×
CFI will not spend a maintenance reserve on anything but its own asset.
×
CFI will not treat reserves as working capital.
×
CFI will not delay reporting bad news to a funder.
Each refusal has a matching control — an approval gate, a financial rule, a governance requirement. A refusal without a control is only an intention.
