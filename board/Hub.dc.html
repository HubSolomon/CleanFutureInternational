The CFI Sustainable Sanitation Hub
The first CFI programme. Components grouped by what each is for — because the three categories fail differently: service failures are visible, continuity failures are silent.
SERVICE — what users come for
• Gender-separated toilet blocks
• Handwashing stations
• Menstrual-hygiene provision
CONTINUITY — what keeps it open
• Borehole and water storage
• Solar power and lighting
• Septic tank or biodigester
REVENUE — what pays for it
• Adult user fee
• Attached kiosk rental
• Advertising panels
