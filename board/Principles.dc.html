Eight operating principles
Eight rules of sequence. Each names what must come first — because almost every failure this model prevents comes from doing the second thing before the first.
1
Community before solution
Understand the problem before selecting an intervention.
2
Engineering before construction
Develop an appropriate technical solution before building.
3
Lifecycle before opening day
Plan operations, maintenance and renewal from the beginning.
4
Local ownership before dependency
Build local capacity and responsibility.
5
Transparency before fundraising
Protect confidence through traceable systems.
6
Evidence before impact claims
Measure outcomes rather than relying on publicity.
7
Sustainability before short-term visibility
Long-term service over launch-day appearance.
8
Proven models before scale
Replicate what evidence shows can work.
Each principle is tested at an approval gate. Where a project cannot satisfy the earlier half, it does not proceed to the later half.
