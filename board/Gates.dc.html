Part IV · Delivery system
Section 13
Twelve approval gates
Each gate is a single question that must be answered affirmatively, with evidence, before a project may proceed. A gate that cannot be passed is not a delay — it is the model working.
0
Problem
Is there a meaningful problem?
1
Evidence
Has the need been sufficiently verified?
2
Feasibility
Can it be solved technically, legally and socially?
3
Sustainability
Can the asset be operated and maintained?
4
Finance
Is there sufficient capital and a credible OPEX plan?
5
Design
Has the technical package been reviewed?
6
Procurement
Is procurement transparent and controlled?
7
Construction
Are quality and progress controlled?
8
Commissioning
Does the infrastructure actually work?
9
Operations
Is a responsible operator and O&M system in place?
10
Impact
Is the intended result being achieved?
11
Scale
Is there sufficient evidence to replicate?
Gates 3 and 4 are the ones most development projects skip. No construction approval without a named operator and a funded operating plan.
