The CFI project test
1 · Is the problem real?
Evidence, not assumption.
2 · Is the solution appropriate?
Fits users, site, environment, operating conditions.
3 · Can it be delivered?
Land, permissions, procurement, capacity.
4 · Will it function after completion?
Operations, staffing, utilities, repairs.
5 · Can we demonstrate impact?
A baseline to compare against.
6 · Can it be improved or replicated?
Every project makes institutional knowledge.
