The three money layers
Three kinds of money with different sources, different rules and different failure modes. The most common cause of trouble is treating them as one pot.
Layer 1
PROJECT CAPITAL
Building the asset
Finite, restricted, ends at commissioning. Grants, CSR, institutional capital.
Fails by: being raised without Layer 2 in place.
Layer 2
HUB OPERATING
Keeping the asset working
Recurring for the asset's life — operator, utilities, maintenance and the renewal reserve.
Fails by: being assumed rather than funded.
Layer 3
CFI CORE
Running the organization
Governance, audit, safeguarding, finance, standards, the registries, fundraising.
Fails by: having no dedicated income at all.
Only two sources reach Layer 3 at any scale: unrestricted giving and cost recovery. Layer 3 must be funded before there is anything to show a funder.
