The initial 24 months
The first two years establish the institution, prove the lifecycle on a single project, and generate the evidence needed to decide whether to replicate.
0–3
MONTHS
Establish the institution
Governance, financial controls, safeguarding, procurement and technical standards. Set the cost-recovery rate. Register the Ghana branch.
Unlocks: a funding case that survives scrutiny
3–6
MONTHS
Measure, then classify
Footfall and willingness-to-pay study at three sites. Baseline and condition survey. Classify each site Type A, B or C.
Unlocks: Gates 1, 2 and 5
6–12
MONTHS
Fund one hub properly
Raise Layer 1 with recovery included and Layer 2 with its renewal provision, as two separate asks. Operator agreement signed first.
Unlocks: Gates 3 and 4 — and construction
12–24
MONTHS
Prove it, then replicate
Commission, operate, and publish real functionality, cost and revenue data. Replace every modelled number with a measured one.
Unlocks: the case for hubs two and three
Fund one hub, not three. Three unproven hubs multiply an unvalidated assumption; one measured for a year turns it into evidence.
