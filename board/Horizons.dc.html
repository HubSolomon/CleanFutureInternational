The ten-year horizons
Four horizons defined by capability, not by date. Figures are planning assumptions, deliberately conservative, revised against evidence at each horizon.
YEAR 1
PROVE
Build the institution and deliver Project 001
~1 asset · 1 programme · 1 country
YEAR 3
REPEAT
Turn one project into a method
3–5 assets · v1 standard designs · first evaluation
YEAR 5
SYSTEMATIZE
Run a country programme, not a series of projects
10–20 assets · 2 programme areas · 1 country assessed
YEAR 10
SCALE
Become an infrastructure development platform
2–4 countries · portfolio reporting · the system as the asset
Year 1 → 3
Project 001 functioning twelve months after commissioning, its costs known and covered.
Year 3 → 5
The method repeated at least three times with comparable results; reserves tested by real repairs.
Year 5 → 10
Country-entry standard documented and met; local leadership in place; portfolio reporting credible externally.
If a horizon's conditions are not met, CFI stays at that horizon and improves. Growing on schedule while the previous horizon is still failing is the pattern this model exists to avoid.
