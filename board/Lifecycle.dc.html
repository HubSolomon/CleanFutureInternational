The CFI project lifecycle
Twelve stages, one continuous chain of accountability. Construction is one stage, not the finish line — and the loop closes: what is measured determines what is discovered next.

Stages 1–3 · Define
Establish that the problem is real, verified and solvable before anything is designed.
Stages 4–6 · Deliver
Fund, build and commission with documented controls and trained operators.
Stages 7–12 · Sustain
Operate, maintain, measure and reinvest — replicate only where evidence supports it.
A project that skips a stage has not saved time. It has moved an unanswered question further downstream.
